PROJECT REPORT - FERABEE LIQUOR PVT LTD,
 GOA
Gold on Black - The Royal Bee | Premium Liquor Brand
1. Executive Summary
FeraBee Liquor Pvt Ltd is a Goa-registered premium liquor manufacturing and hospitality franchise company.
Brand name FERABEE (23=5 numerology) with Royal Bee logo. Company proposes manufacturing of 8
SKUs: Rum, Whisky, Vodka, Gin, Scotch, Tequila, Beer & Wine from Goa distillery/bottling unit under
third-party manufacturing initially, later own distillery. Sales model: Own Bar Franchise - Unlimited Liquor +
Starters concept with Ala-carte food. Year 1: 100% coverage of all Talukas of Goa (12) + Maharashtra (355
Talukas target 50 franchise). Year 2: Expansion to MP, UP, Karnataka, Delhi (200+ franchise).
2. Company Structure

4. Franchise Business Model - Unique in India
Concept: 'FERABEE - Unlimited Spirits & Starters Bar'
Customer pays Fixed Price Rs.999/1499/1999 for 2 hrs unlimited FeraBee liquor (all variants) + unlimited
starters. Food Ala-carte extra 40% margin.
Franchise Investment: 35-45 Lakh (1500 sqft, 60 covers)
Franchise Fee: 7 Lakh (one time) + 6% Royalty + 3% Marketing
FeraBee Supply Margin: Company earns 35-40% on liquor supply to franchise at ex-factory + excise
Target per Franchise Monthly Sale: 8000 Litres IMFL + 5000 Litres Beer = Revenue Rs.45-55
Lakh/month/franchise
Company Net: 12-15 Lakh/month per franchise from supply + royalty
Year 1 Target 50 Franchise = 50 x 12L x 12 = Rs. 72 Cr turnover

6. Licenses & Legal Requirements - Cost Detailed
A) Goa Manufacturing/Bottling (Initial through 3rd party lease):
- Distillery Establishment Licence: Rs.9,20,000/year (Estabizz FL data)
- Bottling Licence: Rs.80,000/year
- Bonded Warehouse: Rs.50,000/year
- Excise Establishment Fee: Rs.2 + plan approvals
- Pollution, FSSAI, Factory Licence: Rs.3-4 Lakh
Total Year 1 Goa Mfg: ~Rs.15-18 Lakh
B) Brand & Label Registration (Most Important):
Goa: Label Registration Fee: Rs.12,000 per pack size per brand (per sources). For 8 brands x 3 pack sizes =
24 labels x 12k = Rs.2,88,000. Renewal Rs.6,000/year.
Karnataka: Rs.1,00,000 per label (if selling there). Punjab ref: Rs.72,000 IMFL label.
Maharashtra: Rs.10,000 per label up to 10 labels, Rs.5,000 beyond.
Budget for All India: Rs.12-18 Lakh Year 1 for 24 labels across 6 states.
C) Franchise Bar Licence (Franchisee bears, but Company support):
Goa FL-2 Retail with Bar: Rs.10,00,000/year
Maharashtra FL-3 Hotel/Restaurant Licence: Rs.6-8 Lakh/year (10% hike 2024), Mumbai 5x higher ~Rs.25-30
Lakh. FL-2 Retail sealed: 15% hike.
Delhi L-1F Wholesale: Rs.5,000 form + 1% of wholesale value licence fee + Label Reg Rs.20k per size
(imported).
Karnataka CL-9 Bar: Rs.12-15 Lakh
MP/UP: Rs.8-12 Lakh
D) Other Licences: Trademark Class 33+32 Registration: Rs.15,000 per class. GST, IEC for Import of Scotch
Malt concentrate, FSSAI for Beer, Excise Permits, Transport Passes.
Total Licence + Label Budget Year 1 (Company side): Rs.35-45 Lakh
