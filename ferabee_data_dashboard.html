⚖️ Legal Token Architecture (Section 142 Defense)

Under Section 142 of the Maharashtra Prohibition Act, 1949, open "unlimited drinking competitions" are illegal. FeraBee operates an airtight, validated compliance architecture:

1. Menu & Board Wording:

Strictly worded as: "Prix-Fixe Multi-Course Dining with Measured Complimentary FeraBee Tasting Session".

2. Measured Token Caps:

Silver (4 tokens = 240ml max), Gold (6 tokens = 360ml max), Platinum (8 tokens). Hard limits prevent intoxication risks.

3. IoT 60ml Electronic Pourers:

Bluetooth-connected optics dispense exact 60ml pegs per token tap directly logged to the excise FLR-3/4 digital register.
